Delete your account

LoopOut: Reel & Shorts Limit — developed by Aasar Mehdi

You can delete your LoopOut account and everything backed up with it at any time. There are two ways: from inside the app, or by email.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app

  1. Open LoopOut.
  2. Go to Settings — tap your profile picture on the Dashboard, or swipe left past the Streaks tab.
  3. Scroll to your account, at the top of the screen.
  4. Tap Delete account and backed-up data.
  5. Confirm by tapping Delete.

Your account and all backed-up data are deleted straight away, and you are signed out. This cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

  1. Email mehdiaasar0@gmail.com from the Google address you signed in with.
  2. Put “Delete my LoopOut account” in the subject line.

We use the sending address to identify your account, so please email from the same address you signed in with. Requests are actioned within 30 days, and usually much sooner.

What gets deleted, and what is kept

DataWhat happens
Your account record — email address, name and profile-picture link from Google Sign-In Deleted immediately
Your backed-up daily totals — per-app video counts, your daily limits, minutes for the most recent day, your daily score, and streak status Deleted immediately
Stats stored on your phone — your counts, limits, streaks and settings Kept on your device. Deleting your account does not touch them. To remove them, go to Android Settings → Apps → LoopOut → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app.

Retention: deleted data is removed from our live database immediately. Residual copies in routine service backups are purged no later than 30 days after deletion. Nothing is retained after that.

LoopOut never uploads screen content, video titles, captions or creator names — only the numeric daily totals listed above. See the privacy policy for full detail.

Just want to stop backing up?

You do not have to delete your account. To stop uploading while keeping what is already backed up, open Settings and turn off the Cloud backup switch. To end your session on the device, tap Sign out.
